Land system, mineral management and GIS

  • Mineral and royalty management: Enverus MineralSoft (revenue tracking, check reconciliation, valuation). This is the usual default for a minerals shop.
  • Land system of record: Quorum On Demand Land (small and mid-size), IFS BOLO/iLandMan (if accounting is on IFS), Pandell LandWorks with Whitestar (GIS-centric), PakEnergy Land (mid-market value), TotaLand (popular with brokers).
  • Grid: Whitestar Texas Original Land Survey grid (about 309,000 abstract polygons statewide) plus NM PLSS. Alternatives are IFS Tobin, the Enverus grid and US Land Grid.
  • GIS: QGIS (free) is enough for a small shop. Use ArcGIS Online or Enterprise once several people need shared ownership maps.
  • Owner contact and heirship: TLOxp or Accurint (needs a permissible-purpose credential), FamilySearch (free), Ancestry and Newspapers.com, Legacy.com obituaries, ClaimItTexas and NM unclaimed property, and DocuSign for ratifications and stipulations.

Recommended stacks

Minerals and land shop, 3–10 people
  • Use TexasFile for name searches and index export. Add CourthouseDirect TVP Plus for plant and legal-description searches and pre-modern images.
  • Use free county portals, BLM MLRS and NMSLO for the latest filings and for federal and state records.
  • Turn on Tracts.co day seats only during active projects. Move to monthly seats once usage is steady.
  • Use MineralSoft for the owned portfolio. Use QGIS with Whitestar for your area of interest, plus free RRC, GLO and BLM layers.
  • Build a thin in-house AI extraction layer (see Automation) on an enterprise LLM plan that doesn't train on your data.
Larger team or fund
  • Run Enverus Courthouse (plants, Instant Analyst, Runsheet Assistant) feeding Tracts.co.
  • Benchmark Energy Domain TitleLab against it as a second source, so you aren't dependent on one vendor.
  • Pick the system of record to match accounting: Quorum, IFS or Pandell.
  • Use Grooper or Collide to load acquisition data rooms. Use ArcGIS Enterprise for shared maps.
  • Give every broker a seat in your system, and set up central de-duplication of title already run, so nobody re-runs a tract the company already owns the work on.
Run a bake-off before you buy any AI title tool

Pick 20 tracts across 4 or 5 counties that already have attorney opinions: a mix of plant counties and late-index counties like Irion or Upton, and at least 3 tracts with heirship breaks. Give each vendor the same legal descriptions. Score them on:

  • Instrument recall: did they find every instrument the attorney examined?
  • Field accuracy: parties, dates, recording references and interest conveyed.
  • Final decimals: exact match to the opinion.
  • Hours of landman review per tract.

Missing an instrument is the expensive failure, so weight recall heaviest.
