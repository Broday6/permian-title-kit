Late-index counties: budget courthouse time

In Irion (1983→), Crockett (1982→), Upton (1984→), Dawson (1970→), Ward (1935→) and Martin (1915→), TexasFile's full index starts late. Use CourthouseDirect's historical books where they exist (Ward, Upton, Dawson, Martin). Otherwise plan on the courthouse or a local plant.

Search the parent county too

Most Permian counties were carved out of Bexar (1876 land-district counties), Tom Green (1887–1903 counties), Presidio (Pecos, and through it Reeves) or El Paso (Culberson). Unorganized counties were attached to a neighbor for recording. Search the parent for anything filed before the organization date. Some counties transcribed the parent's records, which is why an index can start before the county existed.

To confirm a county's current platform, use NETR Online (publicrecords.netronline.com/state/TX/county/<county>) or the clerk's own page. Mineral appraisers (Pritchard & Abbott, Thomas Y. Pickett, Capitol Appraisal and others) are contracted per appraisal district and can change, so check the CAD's mineral roll page.
