Red flagsWhat gets missed, and how it costs you

These are the defects that cause most Permian title surprises. Build each one into your runsheet template as a required check, and into your automation as a rule that raises a flag.

Red flagWhere it hidesWhat to do
Mineral tax suits and tax deedsDistrict court civil index (delinquent ad valorem tax suits against minerals, often against "unknown owners"), sheriff's and tax resale deedsSearch district court for every owner and for the legal description. Check the service and notice in the suit before you rely on the tax deed.
Out-of-county and out-of-state probateThe decedent's home county, not the land's countyUse obituaries and genealogy sources to find the domicile. Record certified copies, or use ancillary probate or an affidavit of heirship.
Unprobated deaths and heirship gapsGrantor disappears from the index, or a later deed comes from "heirs of"Affidavit of heirship from a disinterested witness, family tree, and spouse/community property analysis. In Texas, compute intestate shares under the Estates Code.
Name variants and misindexingInitials, nicknames, married names, "et ux," misspellings, trusts and estates, corporate renamesExpand aliases automatically. Search by legal description where the county allows it. Check the SOS for mergers and name changes.
Carried-forward exceptions"Save and except," "less and except," and "subject to" clauses in a deed 3 or 4 links backRead every vesting deed in full. Each exception becomes its own line in the takeoff until it's resolved.
Double fractions and fixed/floating royalty"1/2 of 1/8 royalty," "1/16 royalty," "1/2 of the royalty" in pre-1980 deeds and reservationsFlag every royalty fraction for attorney review under current Texas law (see the Texas section). Don't let software decide fixed vs floating.
Term royalties and term minerals"For 15 years and as long thereafter as oil, gas or other minerals are produced"Pull the production history for the whole term window, including any cessation, to decide whether the interest expired.
NPRI and poolingA reserved NPRI under a tract in a pooled unit or allocation wellIn Texas the executive can't pool an NPRI without the NPRI owner's joinder. Get ratifications before the pay deck is set.
Unreleased liensOld vendor's liens, deeds of trust covering minerals, judgments, federal tax liensRun lien and judgment indices for each owner. Get releases or subordinations, or document limitations.
State and federal land trapsRelinquishment Act leases not filed with the GLO, University Lands, NMSLO leases, federal assignments not approved by BLM, depth-severed operating rightsCheck the agency record as well as the county record. The agency's approval is part of the chain.
Strips, gores and rights-of-wayRailroad and highway strips, roads, old townsite plats, riverbedsDecide fee vs easement for each strip. Apply strip-and-gore analysis. Check whether a riverbed is state-owned.
Litigation and bankruptcyLis pendens, partition and quiet title suits, PACER bankruptcies of an owner or lesseeSearch lis pendens and district court for every owner, and PACER for entity owners and operators.
Trust and entity authorityTrustee deeds without the trust, terminated trusts with no distribution deeds, dissolved corporationsGet a certificate of trust or the trust instrument and SOS status, and record a distribution or wind-up deed if needed.
