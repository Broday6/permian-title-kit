This calculator works the way your fraction engine should: exact fractions all the way through, rounded to eight places only at the end. Type fractions as written in the instrument (3/16, 1/2 of 1/8, 18.75%). For a horizontal allocation or PSA well, choose how tract participation is set: by acreage, or by completed lateral length in the tract.

Tract participation

NRA is shown on a 1/8 basis (NMA × royalty ÷ 1/8), the convention most mineral buyers price on. Confirm which basis your counterparty uses. An unleased Texas cotenant is paid a share of net proceeds after costs, not a royalty, so price that case separately. A tract fully inside one unit gives the same decimal by acreage as NMA ÷ unit acres × royalty.
